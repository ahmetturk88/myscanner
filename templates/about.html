About MyScanner

MyScanner helps people and small teams check links, files, emails, domains and IP addresses — and shows what was checked, what was found and what remains unknown.

Our Mission

Give anyone a clear, evidence-based answer before they click, open or trust something online — with the reasons behind it.

How We Work

A verdict is only as strong as its evidence. We name every source, report failed checks as unknown rather than safe, and only call something malicious when a reputation source confirms it.

Where We Are

MyScanner is in pre-launch development. Services are being reviewed and tested before public release; features marked “Coming soon” are not available yet.
