Frequently asked

Quick answers.

Is MyScanner available now?

MyScanner is in pre-launch testing. Tools marked “Coming soon”, including the Sandbox, Vulnerability Scanner and Threat Intelligence, are not available yet.

What happens to the data I scan?

Scan results are linked to your account and shown only to you and platform administrators. Some checks query external sources such as reputation databases; each tool names the sources it uses, and optional sources stay off until you choose them.

Does a clean result mean something is safe?

No. A result shows what the available checks observed. A check that could not run is reported as unknown, never as safe.

Do you offer an API or paid plans?

Not yet. Tell us if you need either; your feedback decides what we build next.

How do I report a security issue in MyScanner?

Choose “Security Report” in the form above and describe the issue. Please do not test it against other users' data.
