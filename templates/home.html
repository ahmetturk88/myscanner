CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Good questions. Honest answers.

Does a high score guarantee that a target is safe?

No. Scores summarize observed evidence and coverage. They are not a probability or a guarantee. Review the findings and limitations.

What happens when a source is unavailable?

Reports identify incomplete checks. Available evidence can still be useful, but unavailable evidence is never proof of safety.

Can I scan any website?

Use the tools for public targets you are authorized to inspect. Private and internal destinations are restricted. Some checks may be unavailable or subject to account limits.
