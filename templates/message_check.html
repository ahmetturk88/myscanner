Already tapped the link, paid or shared a code?
  1. Call your bank now on the number on your card or in its app, and ask them to block the card and stop the payment.
  2. If you shared a code, change that account’s password and turn on two-step verification. For WhatsApp: Settings, Account, Two-step verification.
  3. If you installed an app from the message, turn on airplane mode and remove the app, then tell your bank.
  4. Report it to usom.gov.tr and keep screenshots; for money lost, file a complaint with the police or the prosecutor’s office.
