Your data, explained plainly.

02Who we are

MyScanner (“we”, “us”) is an online platform for inspecting links, websites, files, email addresses, domains, IP addresses and QR codes, operated by Ahmed Sairafi in Istanbul, Türkiye. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

The platform is currently in pre-launch testing. Contact: info@myscanners.com.

04How we use it

  • To provide the service: run the inspections you request, save your reports and show your history.
  • To run your account: sign-in, email verification, password resets and security notices.
  • To keep the platform safe: rate limits, daily allowances, abuse prevention and investigating misuse.
  • To answer you: reply to contact messages and security reports.
  • To explain reports and answer security questions: when you press an AI button or write to the AI assistant, generate a plain-language explanation or an answer.

We rely on performing our contract with you, our legitimate interest in a secure service, and, where needed, your consent (for example, optional checks you switch on). We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to anyone.

05What happens to what you scan

  • Links and websites are fetched by our servers from an isolated network, and a page preview is captured in an isolated browser.
  • Files are inspected statically and never executed. The uploaded copy is deleted when the inspection finishes; only the report and file fingerprints (hashes) are kept.
  • Passwords are analysed for strength and are not stored or included in reports. If you choose the breach check, only the first five characters of the password's SHA-1 hash leave our server (k-anonymity), never the password.
  • Email addresses are checked through DNS records. A direct mail-server check runs only if you tick it, and the receiving mail server then sees the address.
  • QR codes are decoded in your browser; you decide whether to inspect the destination.

Please do not scan content you are not allowed to share, such as private documents or links containing secrets.

07Cookies & storage

  • Session cookie: keeps you signed in. Strictly necessary.
  • “Keep me signed in” cookie: only if you choose it.
  • Security token: protects forms against cross-site request forgery.
  • Browser storage: the inspection pages remember your last few targets (never passwords, emails or files) on your device only. You can clear them with the “Clear” button.

We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

08How long we keep data

  • Account and reports: while your account exists. Ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Page previews: deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • AI explanations and questions: kept with the report for up to 30 days (at most your 50 most recent), then deleted. Conversations with the site-wide AI assistant are not stored on our servers; your browser keeps them until you close the tab or start a new conversation.
  • Uploaded files: deleted as soon as the inspection finishes.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited period for security and rotated automatically.
  • Backups: daily database backups are kept for 14 days on our server, and as encrypted copies with our storage provider for 30 days. Data you delete disappears from backups as they roll over.
  • Contact messages: kept as long as needed to handle your request.

09Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK), you can ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your account and data;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent for optional checks at any time.

You can delete your account and its data yourself at any time from your profile (Delete account) or in the MyScanner app. For anything else, email info@myscanners.com from the address on your account. We answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

10How we protect data

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; connections use HTTPS; scanners run in an isolated network that cannot reach internal systems; reports and previews are served only to their owner; sign-in and password-reset requests are rate-limited; and we change-notify you by email when your password changes. No system is perfectly secure: if you find a vulnerability, please report it.

11Children

MyScanner is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.
